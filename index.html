        Good To Know
        
Frequently asked questions

        
Everything you need to know about using this lead-generation front-end for your IT services business.

      

      

        

          Can I target all types of leads?
          

            
Yes. The builder is designed for any map-listed business category such as clinics, restaurants, salons, education, real estate, manufacturers, law firms and more.

          

        

        

          Can I choose any location?
          

            
Yes. Add one city, multiple cities, a specific area, or an entire market region. The preview updates based on whatever locations you enter.

          

        

        

          What details can each lead include?
          

            
You can request business name, niche, phone, website, public email, address, Google rating, review count, map URL, social links, business hours and custom outreach notes.

          

        

        

          Will this page fetch live Google Maps data by itself?
          

            
Not yet. This repository is a static front-end, so the current version generates the lead brief and output layout. Live collection requires a backend integration or approved data provider.

          

        

        

          Can I export leads to Excel or Google Sheets?
          

            
The brief generator already supports those delivery formats in the planning flow. To produce actual export files, connect the form to a backend process that builds the final dataset.

          

        

        

          Is this useful for an IT service company?
          

            
Yes. It is specifically positioned for web development, SEO, ads, software, CRM, AI automation and other IT service teams that want localized B2B outreach lists.

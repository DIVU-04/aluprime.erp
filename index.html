Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your car service.

Do you offer free pick-up and drop?

Yes. We collect your car from your home or office and return it after service at no extra cost across Gota and nearby areas of Ahmedabad.

Which car brands do you service?

Our technicians are trained across all major brands — Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai, Tata, Mahindra, Honda, Toyota, Kia, Volkswagen, Skoda and more.

How do I get a price estimate?

Fill the quick form or message us on WhatsApp with your car details. We share an indicative quote upfront and a final quote after inspection — no hidden charges.

Do you handle insurance claims?

Absolutely. We provide end-to-end assistance with cashless insurance claims and accident repairs, including paperwork and coordination with your insurer.

Are you really open 24×7?

Yes — we offer 24×7 support for breakdowns and emergencies. For routine service, booking a slot in advance helps us serve you faster.

Do you use genuine spare parts?

We use genuine and trusted spare parts sourced from reliable suppliers, and we always confirm with you before replacing any part.
